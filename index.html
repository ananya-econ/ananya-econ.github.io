Working Paper • Seminar Research JEL: J46, L72, O17, F23

Global Value Chains in Extractive Industries

A Case Study of Mining, Labour Precarity, and Value Capture in Odisha

Ananya Pradhan • CISLS, School of Social Sciences, Jawaharlal Nehru University (JNU)
Instructor: Prof. Praveen Jha | Seminar Paper on Global Value Chains

✓ 12-Page Complete Research Paper Available • Empirical Field Evidence

This paper investigates the political economy of Global Value Chains (GVCs) and Global Production Networks (GPNs) within the extractive sector, focusing on bauxite mining and aluminium refining in Odisha, India. Drawing upon theoretical frameworks of value capture, multi-tiered labour subcontracting, and labour control regimes, the study evaluates the impact of transnational capital on local labour processes and livelihood security through an empirical case study of Vedanta Limited's operations in Lanjigarh and surrounding mining clusters. Based on primary fieldwork comprising 35 semi-structured interviews and 5 Focus Group Discussions (FGDs) alongside longitudinal data from the Indian Bureau of Mines (IBM) and official state reports, the findings demonstrate that while mineral extraction integrates the regional economy into global commodity markets, it generates severe socio-economic vulnerabilities. Rather than fostering formal, secure employment, lead firms and their intermediaries utilize multi-layered contract systems to enforce extended work shifts, sub-minimum wages, and unsafe working conditions, suppressing collective bargaining with the aid of state mechanisms.

Master's Thesis • Empirical Econometrics JEL: J16, J31, J71, O15

Gender-Based Wage Discrimination in India

An Oaxaca-Blinder Decomposition Analysis Using PLFS Microdata

Ananya Pradhan • M.A. Labour and Development Studies, Jawaharlal Nehru University (JNU)

✓ M.A. Degree Conferred • Stata Microdata Pipeline

This thesis employs the twofold and threefold Oaxaca-Blinder wage decomposition methodology on Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) microdata to dissect the empirical determinants of the gender wage gap across both formal and informal segments of the Indian economy. By controlling for human capital endowments (education levels, technical training, potential work experience), geographic variables, and occupational/industry classifications, the study partitions the observed raw wage deficit into endowment-explained factors and an unexplained residual commonly attributed to employer discrimination, occupational segregation, and systemic barriers. The findings show that unexplained structural discrimination accounts for a substantial proportion of the gender wage deficit, with informal and rural women facing compounding wage penalties even after controlling for productivity endowments.

Field Monograph • Social Exclusion Studies JEL: J71, O18, Z13

Caste System and Patterns of Discrimination in Rural Labour Markets

An Empirical Investigation in Khamarsahi Village, Kendrapara District, Odisha

Ananya Pradhan • Centre for Study of Discrimination and Exclusion (CSDE), JNU
Submitted to: Prof. Y. Chinna Rao

✓ Primary Field Survey • Village-Level Labour Study

This field monograph examines the structural persistence of caste-based social hierarchies and discriminatory practices within unorganized rural agricultural labour markets. Grounded in primary qualitative and quantitative interviews conducted in Khamarsahi village in coastal Odisha, the paper maps out how caste status intersects with landlessness, occupational segregation, and wage suppression for Scheduled Caste agricultural workers. The analysis traces how customary social sanctions reinforce economic subordination, limiting outside options and depressing bargaining power during peak harvesting cycles.

Theory of Class and Political Economy

Reassessing Classical and Contemporary Frameworks in Developing Formations

Ananya Pradhan • CIPOD, SIS / CISLS, SSS, Jawaharlal Nehru University
Submitted to: Prof. Moushumi Basu

DiD Sentinel

Python • Causal Inference

An auditable toolkit for reviewing standardized staggered-adoption difference-in-differences panel datasets. Validates input contracts, flags treatment-timing anomalies, computes cluster-robust two-way fixed-effects (TWFE) benchmarks, and estimates unadjusted cohort-time DiD aggregates with cluster bootstrap uncertainty.

# Run validation on panel data
$ python3 -m audit.did_sentinel validate panel.csv --out build/validation.json

# Execute full audit & generate report
$ python3 -m audit.did_sentinel audit panel.csv --out build/audit-report
